Biryani

Steps to make Biryani

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for 20–30 minutes.
  2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, salt, red chili, turmeric, and biryani spices.
  3. Slice onions and fry them in oil until golden brown.
  4. Add the marinated chicken and cook until it is partially tender.
  5. Add tomatoes, green chilies, and fresh coriander, then cook until the chicken gravy thickens.
  6. In a separate pot, boil water with salt and whole spices.
  7. Add the soaked rice and cook until it is about 70–80% done, then drain the water.
  8. Spread a layer of rice over the chicken gravy.
  9. Add fried onions, mint, coriander, and a little food coloring or saffron milk if desired.
  10. Cover the pot tightly and cook on low heat for 20–25 minutes (this is called dum).
  11. Gently mix the rice and chicken together.
  12. Serve the hot biryani with raita or salad.

ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken
  • 500 g basmati rice
  • 2 large onions
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 2–3 green chilies
  • ½ cup cooking oil
  • ½ cup fresh coriander
  • ½ cup fresh mint leaves
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 2 tablespoons biryani masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4–5 cloves
  • 2–3 green cardamom pods
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 1 teaspoon black peppercorns
  • A pinch of food coloring or a little saffron milk (optional)
  • Water as needed
